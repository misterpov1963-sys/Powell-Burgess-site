Before the pulpit, before the radio broadcasts, and before the peace, were life's unformed days. Growing up in Jacksonville, Florida, Henry Powell was thrust into severe childhood hardship, tragic loss, and survival on West 2nd Street. A raw, triumphant testament to sovereign redemption.

Meet the Author

Henry L. Powell

Minister, Author, and Storyteller

Henry L. Powell writes from the intersection of painful human reality and triumphant divine grace. Having endured severe childhood hardships in Jacksonville, Florida, his life was altered through an encounter with Christ that propelled him into ministry, radio broadcasts, and teaching.

Through works like Unformed Days, Still Reaching, and The Abiding Word, Henry offers readers a transparent roadmap to spiritual resilience, showing that your scars are never your ruin—they are your testimony.
